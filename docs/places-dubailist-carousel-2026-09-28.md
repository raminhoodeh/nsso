### IYKYK disambiguation

Slide 7 tags `iykykdubai`, a hip-hop and R&B dinner-party organiser. On 28 September its public profile advertised a Saturday dinner party at `salvaje__dubai`. This is not the café branded `iykyk.ae`: the similarly named Google result in Nad Al Sheba 4 (`ChIJowgoxvdDXz4RQwmdBOmwPVs`) was rejected.

The new pin is explicitly a host-venue bookmark at Salvaje, not a promise of an individual event. Its description dates the venue check, asks visitors to confirm current venue, date, entry policy and booking, and explains that Google photos and directions belong to the host. No dated events, opening hours or calendar entries were inferred. Its primary category is Events & Activities, with a Food & Drink tag; the other six new entries are Food & Drink.

### Branch and media safeguards

- Boston Lane refers to its Al Quoz café, not a seasonal market kiosk, another Courtyard tenant or the general Courtyard complex.
- El Primo's Place ID identifies the restaurant, not a mall attraction.
- LahKee is the Al Safa pan-Asian kitchen, not Lah Lah in The Greens.
- No Instagram imagery was republished. New entries use the existing attributed Google Places photo gallery.
- The 159 existing place objects and their order were preserved. Total places: 166; curated records: 87. Existing dated-event records, data expiry metadata, map behaviour and styling were not changed.
- Regression tests cover all seven slide names, exact venue matches, source links, duplicate prevention, the IYKYK distinction and offline regeneration.

## Scope and categorisation

Sessions appear under What's on. Actual exhibition/design displays use Art exhibitions; performances and jam sessions use Live shows; workshops and other dated activities remain discoverable under More events. Festival aliases support both Dubai Future Week and the joined brand spelling Dubai FutureWeek across all three venue pins. New venue-only listings expire after their last published session; the Museum remains a permanent place.

This is a checked programme snapshot, not a live schedule or booking feed. Fresh programme timestamps take precedence over inconsistent cached homepage/search teasers. The older Dubai Future Forum 2025 programme linked from DFF's general events page was not imported. The speaker/exhibitor event terms were not treated as visitor admission rules. Descriptions are concise original paraphrases; where source descriptions were empty, only published title/type/location details were used.

Existing records, including ArtDep, are preserved. The full geocoder/generator was not rerun because it would refresh unrelated locations and prune historical records. Canonical and generated DFW events must agree exactly. Final catalogue totals are 191 places, 113 curated places and 149 stored events; current visible counts additionally apply the existing expiry rules.

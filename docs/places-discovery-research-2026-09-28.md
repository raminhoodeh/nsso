# Places discovery enrichment — 28 September 2026

## What shipped

- Desktop sidebar: 368 → 460px, exactly 25% wider. Still attached to the left edge; map and detail-card dimensions account for it. The existing compact mobile drawer width is retained.
- 15 new fixed-location places, bringing the database to 181 records. Google Places identities and coordinates were checked against official venue addresses; all 15 returned OPERATIONAL. That is a business-status check, not a claim that a venue is open at this moment.
- 11 new dated events, plus a correction to the existing Library Circles record. The agenda is now available for all What's on categories, not just exhibitions, and offers all dates / next seven Dubai calendar dates / current Dubai month.
- 20 source-linked visit guides: a suggested date activity, practical access/booking details, optional venue pairings and a visible source-check date.
- Five editorial date plans. Selecting one narrows the map to its stops and preserves their suggested order. They are not live routes, packages or reservations.

## Research approach

Venue, organiser and official tourism sources were preferred over listicles. Research was checked on 28 September 2026; all event years were verified explicitly. New Google IDs were deduplicated against the existing database. Multiple events share a venue pin; fixed galleries remain discoverable after their exhibitions expire.

Practical facts and editorial interpretation are separated in the UI: “NSSO idea” identifies recommendations, and the source disclosure identifies when details were checked. Published prices are not ticket availability; differing prices or hours are called out or omitted. No bookings, calendar writes or newsletter subscriptions were made.

This is a researched content release, **not an automatic external event feed**. Existing client-clock checks remove expired, cancelled, sold-out or verification-expired event records. This does not detect an organiser changing a future event after the research date. Official links and the checked date remain visible so users can confirm before travelling.

## Corrections, conflicts and exclusions

- **Library Circles:** [official display](https://jameelartscentre.org/whats-on/library-circles-rend-beiruti/) continues 16 September–11 January; the 19 September tour is past. The event now describes the research display and no longer invites users to a past tour.
- **Fiker:** [official FAQ](https://www.fikerinstitute.org/contact-us/faq) lists AED85/day and Monday closure. Paid access is explicit.
- **Jameel:** [visit page](https://jameelartscentre.org/visit/) and [visitor FAQ](https://jameelartscentre.org/visitor-information/) disagree on evening closing time. Retain confirmed free entry and Tuesday closure, but direct users to confirm hours.
- **Yadawei:** price header and VAT-inclusive product copy differ. No single unqualified price is shown.
- **Tashkeel:** access hours differ between facilities pages. Use the booked session's location and timing.
- **XVA:** gallery and café have separate opening schedules. An evening café visit is not presented as gallery access.
- **Commercial galleries:** no blanket free-admission claim without published evidence. Dom and Jameel explicitly publish free entry.
- **Al Thuraya / Ras Al Khor:** not added as currently visitable recommendations because current operation could not be established reliably.
- **Recurring shows:** no future Courtyard Playhouse dates extrapolated from older Wednesdays.
- Existing Google-geocode `meta.expiresAt` reflects the oldest location fetch, not event expiry or the date of this research. It was not advanced to falsely imply the entire dataset had been re-geocoded. Events use their own explicit end/verification timestamps; photos are fetched when a venue is selected.

## Ownership and release checks

New place identities/events live in `dubai-date-curation.json` and the generated map payload; guides and plans are separately maintained in `places-editorial.json`. Date calculations use Asia/Dubai independently of browser timezone. Map pins, group counts and agenda rows share event filtering. Plan order takes precedence over Near me sorting. Pairing links reset incompatible filters before selecting the next venue.

No global styles, shared glass components, Razinflix, authentication or user accounts were modified. Release verification includes source-data regressions, type/lint/build checks and actual browser interactions on desktop and mobile.

Local release checks passed: 60 Node tests, targeted ESLint, TypeScript, production build and 42 browser checks. Browser coverage includes 460px geometry at 1440/1024/901px, all 15 new decoded photo galleries, date-window/pin consistency, all five plans, source/calendar links, saved places, persistent Save/Close while reading long guides, and real touch panning after zooming within the map's geographic bounds. Mobile sizes were 390×844 and 320×568. No application/browser exceptions occurred; one auxiliary Google GetViewportInfo request failed while map tiles, photos and all interactions still passed. Final screenshots were taken after tile fades and drawer transitions settled.

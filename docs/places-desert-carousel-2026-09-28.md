### Hidden

[Official profile](https://www.instagram.com/hidden_dubai/) links the [exact Lisaili site](https://maps.app.goo.gl/phGKeRBfAJfz67nD6). Its [25 September announcement](https://www.instagram.com/hidden_dubai/reel/DdtWRkGpXPN/) announces a return on 1 October. The public post timestamp is `2026-09-25T11:28:47Z`, grounding the year as 2026. This is an announced plan, not a guarantee that the café has reopened or a confirmation of daily hours.

The [neighbouring operator’s location announcement](https://arabian-adventures.presspage.com/new-desert-dining-and-entertainment-destination-the-fort--lisaili-opens-in-dubai/) helps disambiguate the site. Its Fort admission, performances and booking policy do not transfer to Hidden.

### LIMITED

The [official profile](https://www.instagram.com/limited.dubai/) explicitly says temporarily closed and links [Al Marmoom Lakes](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VDCESNeXbhzowNxv8). A February 2026 Ramadan private-event offer is historical; it is not proof of present public access or reopening. No new reopening date was verified.

### Parker’s

A reservation link does not establish available dates. Its [published desert menu](https://byparkers.com/uae/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/04/Desert-Menu.pdf) is background inspiration, not a current-price guarantee.

### One Degree

### My Space

The [official profile](https://www.instagram.com/myspace.cafe/) links [My Space Desert (Nazwa)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/unsFF2YrRJkJ4QiR9?g_st=ipc) separately from Aljada. It lists +971 52 729 6666 for the desert branch. Recent Aljada 24/7 and parking promotions do not apply to Nazwa. The desert address belongs to Sharjah, despite the carousel’s Dubai-themed cover.

### The Uncommon: historical pin, not an invented current branch

The [official website](https://theuncommon.ae/pages/about-us) distinguishes Al Marmoom from Al Fayah, DIFC, Beach Edition and other ventures. [Visit Dubai](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/articles/dubais-desert-activities) embeds historical Google ID `ChIJ_TSBSwCDXz4Ra9z_COwM-MA`, but Place Details returned 404 and public Maps no longer supplies a venue panel. The default Dubai viewport is not location evidence.

The [official archived DSF map](https://dsfmap.visitdubai.com/) still loads [public location data](https://dsfmap.visitdubai.com/assets/maps/dsfinteractive.json). Three The Uncommon X DSF entries point to [original directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Brm66n6wVo8kavdKA?g_st=iw), which preserve the historical feature ID and the query `R9Q4+PR The Uncommon - Dubai`. Full plus code `7HPQR9Q4+PR` resolves to Al Lisaili and decodes to 24.8393125, 55.3570625 (an approximately 14-metre cell).

That is sufficient to bookmark the former site, not to assert a current entrance, parking route or reopening. The record has a null Google Place ID, an explicit official-source resolution override and an unconfirmed-season notice. It does not issue broken Google photo requests or substitute DIFC photos. Its visit guide explains the absence of a current gallery. The expired DSF 2024–25 programme was not added to the current agenda.

## Product safeguards

- Six new place records and six visit guides; 187 total records / 108 curated records. Existing 181 records and 32 event records are unchanged.
- All six remain visible under Eat & drink → Cafés & coffee, saveable and mapped. No new dated events or calendar bookings were inferred.
- Reported closures show in list rows and detail panels with the snapshot date. The Uncommon has a distinct official-source “Seasonal reopening unconfirmed” notice.
- Surprise us excludes reported closures and explicitly unconfirmed seasons; an all-ineligible selection reports the reason without selecting a closed fallback.
- No opening-hours, pet policy, price, minimum-spend, parking-safety or off-road-access guarantees are inferred from old posts. Ask the venue about arrival and use its current instructions.
- Instagram images were inspected as research, not republished. No browser keys or transient photo URLs are stored in these records.
- Existing Google cache expiry metadata is preserved; the new research does not imply every older pin was reverified.
